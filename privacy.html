World leaderboard privacy policy and terms

Real Roads Atlas · effective 1 February 2027 · version 1

Draft: the game's administrator fills in the controller's name and contact address before the world leaderboard goes live.

In short

  • The game works without an account. Your records, friends and profile stay on your device until you join the world leaderboard.
  • When you join, your nickname, times and replays go to our server in the EU and show on a public leaderboard.
  • We don't collect your real name, email, phone number, location, contacts or advertising ID. No ads, no tracking, and we don't sell data.
  • You can delete your data at any time in the game's Settings.
  • Use a nickname, not your real name. If you're under 15, ask a parent first.

Controller

The data controller is the administrator of the game Real Roads Atlas. Name and contact address: filled in by the administrator before launch.

What data and why

DataWhy
Player ID (12 characters) and public key, generated randomly on your deviceSigning what you send, so nobody can send or delete a time in your name.
Nickname (3–16 characters)Showing it on the leaderboard.
Track, mode, weather and driving physicsSeparate leaderboards for the same conditions.
Time, split times, car, upgrades and assist levelRanking and display.
Replay of the run (input per step) and ghost (car positions)Checking that the time is real. The ghosts of the top 100 times are public, so others can race against them.
Game version and browser engine family (not the full browser string)Replaying the run with the same code.
Time sent and check statusRunning the checks.
Nickname reports (reporter's ID, time)Hiding unsuitable nicknames.

The server sees your IP address with every request (that's how the web works). It keeps only a hash of it, salted with a salt that changes daily, to limit how often data can be sent, and deletes it after 48 hours. We don't derive your location from it.

Drivers you hide on the leaderboard and your friends list stay on your device and are never sent to the server.

Legal basis

  • Providing the service you choose by joining (leaderboard, nickname, ghost).
  • Legitimate interest in protection against cheating and abuse (replay checks, hashed IP address, nickname reports).

Who processes the data

  • Server host: the server, database and replays are in the EU; the host processes data only on our instructions (data processing agreement).
  • Automated checking service: replays are re-run on an automated code testing service. It receives the replays without nicknames or IDs. This service may be outside the EU (including the US); the transfer is protected by standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

We don't share data with anyone else and we don't sell it.

How long we keep it

  • A time stays as long as it's on the leaderboard or until you delete it.
  • A player who sends no time for 24 months is deleted.
  • Hashed IP address: 48 hours. Database backups: up to 30 days.

Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification (change your nickname in Settings), erasure and objection, and the right to lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia or the supervisory authority in your country.

Deleting your data

  • In the game: Settings → World leaderboard → Delete my world leaderboard data. Your nickname, times and replays are deleted at once.
  • Without your device: write to the contact address above with your nickname and player ID (the game shows it in Settings). We delete within 30 days.
  • Leaving the leaderboard (Leave) stops sending; the records on your device stay.

Young players

In Slovenia you can join on your own from the age of 15. If you're younger, ask a parent or guardian first.

Terms of use

  • Offensive nicknames, nicknames that impersonate others, and other people's real names are not allowed.
  • Cheating (a modified game, forged replays) and automated requests are not allowed.
  • The administrator may hide a nickname, delete a time, or stop a player who breaks these terms from sending times.
  • The leaderboard is free and provided as is; it may change or stop.

Changes

A changed policy is published on this page with a new version and date.
