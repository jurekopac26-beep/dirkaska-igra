THE SIX VERSIONS (my choice; each clearly different, each with real drawers):
  1 MEHANIČNA OMARA ZA ORODJE: two mechanic's roll cabinets (navy with the yellow line, generic, aluminium pulls, castors), five drawers
    each pulled out in a staircase, every drawer with a two-tone foam insert (dark grey over yellow) and the items in their cut-outs; the
    tops' parts on a ribbed rubber mat on each cabinet's top.
  2 PREDALNIK ZA NAČRTE: one long low plan-chest across the whole width (the gift's gap gone), many wide shallow drawers, four or five
    pulled out to different depths, the items lying flat on dark felt/foam in shaped recesses, small name tags; the tops' parts in the
    top drawers too (no parts on the top).
  3 DELOVNI PULT S PREDALI: a heavy workbench (steel top with a rubber mat holding the tops' parts, or a wooden top) over two banks of deep
    drawers, the drawers pulled out, deep compartments with steel dividers, the items standing/lying in the compartments.
  4 LOČNA OMARA S PRAVIMI PREDALI: the cabinets following the ring's arc (as the regal), but done right: each drawer a real tapered box
    along the arc, staircase-pulled, foam inserts with cut-outs; the arc only in the cabinet body, the drawers' insides straight.
  5 OMARA S PLASTIČNIMI ZABOJČKI: a small-parts bin cabinet: rows of pull-out bins (sturdy plastic, in the game's colours) and drawers;
    each element in its own bin pulled out, the bin's front lip and label in front of it; the bigger parts in deep drawers below.
  6 SKRINJA Z ODPRTIM POKROVOM IN PREDALI: each cabinet is a top chest with its lid open (the lid's inside lit, a mirror-like strip) over a
    roll cabinet: the tops' parts in the chest's top tray (in foam), the other groups in the drawers below, staircase-pulled.
