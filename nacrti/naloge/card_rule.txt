NEW RULE FROM THE USER FOR EVERY PICTURE WITH A CARD (okvirček / menu): "Meni, ki se odpre, naj ne prekriva avtomobila v garaži, sedaj mu
prekriva streho." = The card that opens must NOT cover the car; today it covers the car's roof.
HOW: in every frame with a card, find the car's silhouette on screen (an ID pass of the car, or the projected bounding box of its body)
and keep the card's whole box clear of it with at least 8 CSS px of gap. In the first view the car's roof is at about y 380-410 (412x915):
the card must end above it. Make the card compact so it fits: smaller row height (about 28-30 px), the stat line on the same row or
smaller, tabs and buttons tighter, max width about 300 px; if a menu has more options than fit, show a scrolling list (4-5 rows visible,
a fade and a small scroll mark at the bottom) instead of a taller card. If the camera has moved closer (close-up frames), place the card
where the car is not (top or bottom of the screen, whichever is free), never over the car or the part being shown. The flag and the tap
ring may sit near the element as before. Re-render the frames with cards that you already made, and say in your report the gap you
measured per picture (card box vs. the car's silhouette).
